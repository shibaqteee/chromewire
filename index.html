CHROMEWIRE: Privacy Policy

Effective: 18 October 2026

CHROMEWIRE ("the app") is a theme pack that delivers wallpaper presets to the KLWP app made by Kustom Industries.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not collect, store or share personal or device data.

Licence check

The app may ask Google Play to confirm that it was purchased (Google Play Licensing). That request goes between your device and Google only. See Google's privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Data shown by the themes

The themes display your time, weather, calendar events, music, notifications and news feeds. KLWP reads that data on your device under the permissions you grant to KLWP. It is not sent to us. KLWP's own privacy policy applies to KLWP.

News feeds

If you use the built-in news pages, KLWP downloads the RSS feeds you choose directly from their publishers.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
